# Vintage Website Pro — Vintage Paper Texture Generator Pro

A museum-grade, high-performance web application for procedurally generating authentic vintage paper textures, handmade fibrous parchment, antique deckle edges, and commercial print-ready digital backdrops.

## ✨ Highlights & Key Features

- **100 Advanced Paper Categories & 2,000 Presets**: From Handmade Cotton Fiber & Japanese Washi to Distressed Parchment, Leatherette, Papyrus, and Gold Foil Specialty Papers.
- **100 Recommended Color Palettes**: 10 curated collections (Warm & Neutral, Earthy & Organic, Vintage & Antique, Luxury, Dark & Dramatic, etc.).
- **100 Advanced Paper Edge Styles**: 10 procedural edge groups (Deckle edges, torn & ripped, fibrous fringe, scorched combustion zones, irregular jagged, scalloped artistic, etc.).
- **Interactive Multi-Cell Artboards**:
  - **Figma / Adobe-Style Design Grab & Selection**: Select, inspect, and hover designs with high-contrast outlines and handles.
  - **Smooth Drag-and-Drop Reordering**: Drag and swap designs across artboard cells with live previews and undo/redo history (`Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Y`).
  - **Keyboard Delete & Backspace**: Instant design removal using the keyboard with minimum artboard safety guards.
- **Fluid Canvas Navigation**:
  - **Cursor-Centred Mouse Wheel Zoom**: 15% to 1000% focal zoom.
  - **Spacebar + Left Mouse Pan**: Effortlessly pan the stage anywhere.
  - **⛶ Expand Theater Mode**: +305% canvas expansion for pixel-level inspection of pulp, fibers, and deckle edges.
- **120 FPS High-Speed Performance**:
  - V8 JIT vectorized pixel loops.
  - Offscreen double-buffering and preset thumbnail bitmap caching.
  - Hardware GPU layer promotion (`contain: layout paint`).
- **All-Device Responsiveness**:
  - Deep-audited across 10 device viewports (320px to 1920px) with 0px horizontal overflow.
  - Mobile Dual-Mode Switcher (`📐 Tools` vs `⚙️ Properties`), touch-optimised 22px slider handles, and table wrappers.
- **Commercial Production Export Center**:
  - High-res raster exports (PNG with 600 DPI pHYs chunks, JPEG with APP0 300 DPI + Adobe CMYK Coated FOGRA39 ICC profiles).
  - Scalable vector exports (SVG, EPS, AI, and Adobe Illustrator ExtendScript `.jsx`).
  - Automated PKZIP archive bundling.
  - Commercial stock metadata generator (Adobe Stock, Freepik, Etsy).
- **100% Self-Contained**: Zero external CDNs or third-party dependencies.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Simply open `Vintage Paper Texture Generator Pro.html` in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Safari).
